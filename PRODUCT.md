# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Authorized Arabic-speaking electrical ERP staff maintaining customer, supplier and product master data and preparing controlled quotations at desktop workstations, with mobile access for reference.

## Product Purpose
Maintain accurate, permission-controlled master data in one modular ERP. Customer identity remains usable independently of sales and accounting.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, professional. The six current quotation screenshots and M04 Q-01 through Q-29 prompt in docs/client_ui_ref govern Sales Quotations. Existing M01–M03 surfaces remain the reusable baseline.

## Anti-references
Static mockups with invented balances, approvals, invoices, or copied screenshot identities. Separate frontend stacks and decorative dashboard content.

## Design Principles
Reuse the ERP shell and controls. Keep workflows explicit. Render real persisted data. Expose unavailable integration states honestly. Preserve business ownership and audit history.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Arabic RTL and existing English LTR architecture; keyboard-operable controls, visible validation, responsive layouts, reduced motion and readable status labels.
