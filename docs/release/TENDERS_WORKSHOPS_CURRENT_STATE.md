## Reuse and obsolete assumptions

M09 is real and must be extended, not replaced. M08 is field service and cannot stand in for workshop custody. The `customer_owned` boolean is not a company asset registry. System `SC/TN/TB` prefixes are shared numbering capabilities, not ServiceContracts/Tender implementations. M15 is Attendance and is absent; it is not Reporting. No duplicate tender implementation was found. The combined estimate decision is an incomplete business step, not a second Sales engine to retain alongside a replacement.

Stage B will place tender functionality within the existing Projects owner with distinct `M07.tender_*` permissions and source identities; no eighteenth bounded owner or M07 DeliveryReturns alias is introduced. M09 will use a minimal source-specific quotation integration with existing M02 prices/M11 tax/M17 documents and keep invoice/AR in M12. Contract destinations and coverage remain unavailable while M10 is absent. No real workshop names, rates, margin, guarantees, submissions, financial activation or company balances will be fabricated.
